Two scales, one visual language

Our icon and pictogram collections express Skunkworks capabilities through clear, consistent geometry. Icons help people navigate interfaces; pictograms illustrate broader ideas across presentations, websites, campaigns and learning materials.

Using these assets

  • Keep each SVG’s viewBox and proportions; scale uniformly and leave breathing room around the artwork.
  • Icons use currentColor: inline the SVG to inherit interface text colour. When used as an image, it renders black by default; use it on a light surface or set a suitable colour inside a local copy.
  • Use icons at 24px or larger, and provide at least a 44px interaction target for icon-only controls.
  • Pictograms are illustrative, not logos or substitutes for functional interface icons. Start at 96px and inspect legibility at the intended size.
  • Pair meaningful visuals with clear labels or equivalent alternative text. Hide decorative artwork from assistive technology.
  • Check contrast in light and dark themes. Place the coloured pictograms on White or Off White surfaces and do not stretch, add effects or alter individual elements.
