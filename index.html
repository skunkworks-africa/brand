05 / Governance

Consistency
is intentional.

All Skunkworks properties should use approved assets, shared foundations and accessible interaction patterns. New regional or product identities should inherit the core brand before adding justified, documented extensions.

  • Use source-controlled, versioned assets and tokens.
  • Validate responsive behaviour and both colour themes.
  • Review new patterns for accessibility and brand fidelity.
  • Obtain internal approval before releasing new variants or lockups.
