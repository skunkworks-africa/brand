Choose a card for the job

Card patterns by user need
User needs toPatternInclude
Understand a priority serviceFeatured serviceOutcome, useful media and one next step.
Evaluate an offerOffer and pricingCurrency, amount, billing interval, scope and material terms.
Find a resourceNavigationA specific title, short summary and one destination.
Know what happens nextStatus and progressA readable state, progress when measurable and a next action.
Interpret a key numberMetricValue, unit, period and comparable baseline.
Take action on a recordTaskTask, owner, due date and separate controls for separate actions.
Choose among optionsSelectable cardsA group label, native controls and an identifiable selected state.

Use a data table when people need to compare the same fields across many items. A list works well for short names and links.

Structure and layout

Put the title and decision details before the action. Use a consistent field order across comparable cards. Let sk-card-grid reflow with the available width and keep important labels and prices visible in narrow layouts.

Links and actions

Use one anchor for a navigation card. For a card with several actions, use separate links and buttons with distinct names. Keep focus visible on the exact element someone can activate.

States and feedback

Use readable status text and a concrete next step. Keep checked or pressed states in native controls or ARIA. Announce a meaningful asynchronous change once, and give people a retry or recovery path where appropriate.

Images and publishing

Use media when it helps identify or understand the item. Keep dimensions and crops consistent. Give informative images concise alternative text and use empty alt text for decorative images. Distinguish sample prices and metrics from your live offers and data.

Use unique IDs for each copy of a card. The status and save demos use preview JavaScript that you can adapt to your application's data and actions.
