Motion tokens

Choose a duration based on the size of the change. Use easing to control how the motion starts and stops. The standard values are a good starting point for most controls.

Motion values generated from tokens.css
TokenValueWhen to use it
--sk-motion-duration-instant0msApply an immediate change, for example when showing an error or reducing motion.
--sk-motion-duration-quick100msUse for button presses and other small changes that follow an action.
--sk-motion-duration-standard160msUse for a disclosure or short piece of feedback.
--sk-motion-duration-expressive240msUse for the entrance of a dialog or similarly substantial surface.
--sk-motion-duration-indicator800msSet the length of one activity indicator rotation.
--sk-motion-ease-standardcubic-bezier(.2,0,.2,1)Move smoothly between two visible states.
--sk-motion-ease-entercubic-bezier(0,0,.2,1)Bring a new surface into view and let it settle quickly.
--sk-motion-ease-exitcubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)Let a departing surface move away quickly when an exit effect helps.
--sk-motion-ease-linearlinearKeep a steady pace for an activity indicator.
--sk-motion-distance-overlay8pxLimit the vertical travel of a dialog on entry.

These values are shared by the light and dark themes. See the full design token reference.

Choose motion for the component

Buttons and switches
Show focus immediately. Use quick press feedback on the control and show whether it is selected, busy or complete with text or a persistent state.
Accordions, menus and tabs
Keep the trigger stable and reveal related content nearby. Use a short fade and preserve keyboard behaviour, reading order and focus.
Dialogs and drawers
Use a short entrance from the edge where the surface appears. Set focus as soon as the surface opens and return it to the trigger when it closes.
Form errors and notifications
Show the message immediately. Keep it readable until users can act. Pair visual emphasis with actionable text, especially in form validation and lead capture.
Loading, progress and skeletons
Show a status message for work with unknown duration. Use a labelled progress bar when you can measure progress. Reserve the space the result will occupy and keep decorative placeholders still.
Page changes and scrolling
Prioritise orientation with headings, focus and navigation. Use a static layout when motion adds no information. Avoid parallax, sudden zooms and effects that take over scrolling.

Respect reduced motion

Apply nonessential effects inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). Use the final state, focus and status text immediately when motion is reduced. The preview control on this page lets you check those states without changing your device settings.

  • Keep open, closed, pending, error and complete states clear when animations are off. Keep an accessible name or message for every status.
  • Provide a way to pause, stop or hide nonessential motion that starts automatically, lasts more than five seconds and appears alongside other content. Avoid flashing and check auto-updating content separately.
  • Keep navigation, dismissal and retry responsive during an effect. Avoid using an animation event to unlock the next step.

See W3C guidance on animation from interactions and pause, stop and hide.

Use motion in your project

Load tokens.css, components.css and the reusable motion styles. Add sk-motion-press to a button, sk-motion-enter to content that appears, or sk-motion-dialog to a native dialog. A sk-motion-spinner can accompany visible status text while work is pending.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/components.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/motion.css">

<details class="sk-accordion">
  <summary>Assessment details</summary>
  <div class="sk-motion-enter"><p>Review the assessment scope.</p></div>
</details>

Set data-sk-motion="reduced" on a containing element if your product provides a separate motion preference. The system preference still applies.

Review before release

  • Check keyboard and touch interactions, reversed actions, repeated use and focus after dismissal.
  • Check both themes and reduced motion, including loading, error, retry and success.
  • Animate specific properties like opacity and transform. Profile on a lower-powered device and remove effects that make the task feel slower.
